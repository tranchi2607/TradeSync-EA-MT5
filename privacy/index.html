TradeSync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

App: TradeSync (MT5 account management & copy trading)
Developer: TradeSync (matches the name on Google Play)
Privacy contact email: privacy@tradesync.site

This policy describes how the TradeSync app collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the app. By using TradeSync, you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

CategoryData
User accountEmail, password (hashed — we do NOT store passwords in plain text), device name, device identifier (a UUID generated by the app).
MT5 trading accountsAccount number, broker name, server, balance, equity, open orders/positions and trade history — sent by the EA in READ-ONLY mode.
Copy tradingCopy relationships between accounts, copy settings, master notes.
NotificationsTrading events (open/close orders, disconnects, summaries) used to display notifications.
TechnicalApp/EA version, the IP address of the machine running the EA (to group by VPS), technical error logs.

We do not collect bank card details. All in-app payments are processed by Google Play Billing; we only receive the purchase status (purchased / not purchased).

2. How we use data

  • Display and manage your MT5 accounts.
  • Perform copy trading between accounts you configure.
  • Send notifications about trading events.
  • Provide the AI Assistant feature at your request.
  • Authenticate sign-in, protect security and prevent abuse.
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues.

3. Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell personal data. Data is only processed by the service providers required to operate the app:

  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchase payments.
  • AI provider (Anthropic — Claude) — ONLY when you use the AI Assistant: your request content and the necessary context are sent for processing.
  • Server / CDN infrastructure — secure storage and data transfer.

We do not share your trading data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Data protection

  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage.
  • The EA has READ-ONLY access to accounts for syncing; copy orders are placed only when you enable them.

5. Data retention

We keep account data for as long as you use the service. Notifications and trade snapshots are kept temporarily and cleaned up automatically on a schedule. When you delete your account/data, we remove the related data within a reasonable time.

6. Account & data deletion

You can:

  • Unlink each MT5 account directly in the app (that account's data is removed from the server).
  • Request deletion of your entire user account and related data by emailing privacy@tradesync.site from your registered email. We process requests within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

TradeSync is not intended for people under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted on this page along with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Any privacy questions: privacy@tradesync.site
